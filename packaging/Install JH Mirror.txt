JH Mirror — your Android phone on your Mac
Developed by Johirul Hoq Akash

ONE APP
All core tools, the Android companion and an offline Quick Start guide are inside
JH Mirror.app. No Homebrew, Python, Java, Android SDK or other JH Mirror downloads
are needed. OBS and BlackHole are optional call add-ons only.

INSTALL
If upgrading, quit the existing JH Mirror when you have finished using the phone.
1. Drag "JH Mirror" onto the Applications folder.
2. Open JH Mirror from Applications.
   The first time, macOS may say it cannot check the app (it is not from the App Store).
   Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" next to JH Mirror.
   First try opening the app, then use Open Anyway and confirm Open.
3. Allow notifications and local network access when macOS asks.

PAIR YOUR PHONE (once)
For wireless pairing, Android 11+ and the same Wi-Fi are required.
Android 8–10: enable USB debugging, connect USB, approve this Mac on the phone,
and choose Use USB-connected phone in JH Mirror.
1. On the phone: Settings → About phone → Software information → tap "Build number" 7 times
   (this turns on Developer options).
2. Settings → Developer options → Wireless debugging → turn it on.
3. Tap "Pair device with QR code" and scan the code JH Mirror shows.

That's it. JH Mirror installs its phone app, grants its permissions, and connects by itself:
screen mirroring, notifications (click, reply, dismiss), files, clipboard and drag & drop.
After a phone restart it reconnects on its own; the menu bar icon has a Reconnect button.

PHONE SCREEN OFF
On Android 14+, the Phone Tools screen button also blocks physical phone touch.
Press the phone power button or stop mirroring to restore touch. A lost Mac connection
releases the touch guard within eight seconds.

PHONE TOOLS AND APPS
Click the small white arrow at the mirror's top-right to expand/collapse tools.
App Library opens a simple four-column app drawer beside the mirror. Click an icon
to open its app; right-click for Uninstall with confirmation. Click App Library again
or press Escape to close the drawer. System apps and the active launcher/keyboard
are protected. The saved list updates once daily when used; click its small refresh
icon for an immediate update. Reopening or restarting reuses today's saved list.
Activity pulses prevent idle sleep during visible mirroring and while a dark mirror
is hidden/minimized. A deliberately dark phone stays dark after closing the mirror.
Turn it on with the phone power button or Turn Phone Screen On in the JH Mirror menu.
Closing releases touch protection and allows Android to sleep. Unlock once if the phone
was already locked; your phone timeout, PIN and biometric settings are unchanged.

PHONE FILES AND MEDIA
Open the folder or photo icon in Phone Tools. Browse shared storage, Downloads,
Camera, Pictures, Videos, Music and Documents. Photos/videos have on-demand previews.
Drag Mac files or folders into the browser to send to the current folder. In a media
view, uploads go to Downloads. Select phone items and Save to Mac, or drag them into
Finder. The transfer list shows progress and speed. Pause keeps partial copies;
Resume continues from the saved byte position, including after an app restart.
Completed copies are SHA-256 verified. Existing files are kept; colliding names get
a number. Original files are not deleted. Android-protected app storage is excluded.
A brief disconnect retries up to three times. Actual speed depends on the connection
and storage. No extra file-access permission or network server is required.

PHONE CAMERA AND MICROPHONE FOR CALLS
Open Help → Setup & Add-ons for detected status, downloads and setup help.
Install only the optional add-ons you need:
- Camera: OBS Studio (Apple silicon) — https://obsproject.com/download
  Setup: https://obsproject.com/kb/virtual-camera-troubleshooting
- Microphone: BlackHole 2ch — https://existential.audio/blackhole/
  Setup: https://github.com/ExistentialAudio/BlackHole#installation-instructions
 Approve the OBS camera extension, stop Virtual Camera, then quit OBS.
Direct mode needs no OBS window, scenes, server or password. BlackHole is a background
driver; no audio app needs to stay open. Click Phone Camera in the menu bar to start a
live preview. Choose Front/Rear, rotate 90 degrees or Mirror. Minimize to keep video
running during a call; closing the window stops it. Phone Mic starts separately.
In your calling app choose OBS Virtual Camera and BlackHole 2ch. Keep speakers as your
usual Mac/headphone output. Camera needs Android 12+. Phone capture is always manual.

Help → Quick Start opens the offline setup guide bundled inside the app.
Requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. The app states its minimum version.
