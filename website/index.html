See it work in under two minutes

Pairing, a reply from a notification, a photo dragged into Finder. Real app, nothing staged.

Like Universal Clipboard

Copy on your phone. Paste on your Mac.

Copy a link, an address or a photo on your phone, then press ⌘V in any Mac app. It's already there. Going the other way, ⌘V in the phone window pastes your Mac's text into the phone.

  • Text and images copy across automatically
  • Anything the phone marks sensitive, like passwords, is skipped
  • Clipboard history sits in the menu bar
  • A Copy to Mac tile in Quick Settings for one‑off sends

Like iPhone notifications on Mac

Answer your phone's messages from your Mac

Texts, chats and every other app's alerts show up as normal Mac notifications, with the phone's own buttons. Type a reply in the banner and your phone sends it. Dismiss it on the Mac and it's gone from the phone too.

  • Reply, Mark as read and Dismiss right in the banner
  • Click a notification to open that app in the phone window
  • Follows macOS Focus and hides sensitive content

Like AirDrop, for Android

Move files between Android and Mac without a cable

Open your phone's storage in a Mac window. Browse Downloads, DCIM, Documents and every shared folder, search by name, make new folders, then drag files into Finder or drop Mac files onto the phone.

  • Every file is checked with SHA‑256
  • Big transfers pause and resume, even after a restart
  • Never overwrites a file that's already there
  • No size limit and no cloud upload

Like Photos on Mac

All your photos, videos and music on a bigger screen

Photos, Videos and Audio each get their own tab, with thumbnails that load as you scroll. Press Space to preview, swipe to the next one, ⌘C to copy a photo, or save the full‑quality original to your Mac.

  • Originals arrive untouched, never recompressed
  • Videos play and songs preview without leaving the window
  • Free up space: delete phone photos after a confirm step

Like iPhone Mirroring

Your phone in a Mac window, with sound

Reply in any app, check a delivery or finish a level using your Mac's keyboard and trackpad, while the phone stays in your bag. Its sound plays through the Mac's speakers.

  • Streams over Wi‑Fi in real time
  • A side strip for clipboard, files, media and apps
  • ⌘1 phone, ⌘2 media, ⌘3 files

Pocket mode

Turn the phone's screen off and lock its touchscreen while you keep using it from the Mac. No accidental taps in your pocket, and your PIN and lock settings stay as they are. Android 14 and newer.

Like Continuity Camera

Use your Android phone as a Mac webcam

Your phone's camera is better than the one above your screen. Start it from the menu bar, then pick it in your video call app like any other camera. Add the phone's microphone too.

  • Front or rear camera, switched with one click
  • Rotate and mirror live, mid‑call
  • Nothing is recorded; the preview is deleted when you stop
  • Android 12+, with the free OBS camera and BlackHole add‑ons

Pair once. It takes about a minute.

No cable, no Android SDK, and nothing to install by hand on the phone.

  1. Install JH Mirror

    Drag it to Applications. The first time, macOS may ask you to allow it in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

  2. Turn on Developer options

    On the phone: Settings → About phone → Software information, then tap Build number seven times.

  3. Switch on Wireless debugging

    It's inside Developer options. Keep the phone and the Mac on the same Wi‑Fi.

  4. Scan the code

    Tap Pair device with QR code and scan the code on your Mac. A six‑digit code works too.

Then JH Mirror takes over

It installs its small phone app, sets the permissions it needs over the secure connection and opens your phone's screen. You don't dig through any phone settings.

On Android 8 to 10, connect a USB cable once, allow USB debugging and choose Use USB‑connected phone.

Your phone stays between you and your Mac

  • Never leaves your network

    No account and no cloud server. The Mac and the phone talk directly.

  • Encrypted

    Certificate‑pinned TLS with a random 256‑bit token, on top of Android's own authorized debugging link.

  • Camera only when you ask

    The camera and microphone start only when you press them. Video is never recorded.

  • Easy to switch off

    Unpair the Mac in Wireless debugging and uninstall the phone app. Access ends there.

Questions people ask before they download

How do I connect my Android phone to my Mac?

Install JH Mirror on the Mac. On the phone, turn on Developer options and Wireless debugging, tap Pair device with QR code and scan the code JH Mirror shows. Keep both on the same Wi‑Fi. JH Mirror installs its small phone app and connects by itself.

Is JH Mirror free?

Yes. Every feature is free, with no trial, no paid tier, no ads and no account.

Is JH Mirror open source?

Yes. The Mac app and the phone app are open source, so anyone can read the code, check what it does and build it themselves. Screen mirroring is built on scrcpy, which is open source under the Apache 2.0 license.

Can I AirDrop from Android to Mac?

Most Android phones can't use AirDrop. JH Mirror does the same everyday job: drag photos and files from the phone into Finder, or drop Mac files onto the phone, over your Wi‑Fi.

Is this an Android File Transfer alternative for Mac?

Yes. It opens the phone's storage in a Mac window where you can browse folders, search, create folders and copy files both ways, without a USB cable. Every transfer is checked with SHA‑256 and large files resume after a restart.

Can I copy and paste between Android and Mac?

Yes. Text or an image you copy on the phone is ready to paste on the Mac with ⌘V. Copies the phone marks as sensitive, such as passwords, are skipped. In the phone window, ⌘V pastes Mac text into the phone.

Can I use my Android phone as a webcam on my Mac?

Yes, on Android 12 and newer. Start Phone Camera from the menu bar, pick the front or rear camera, then choose the OBS Virtual Camera in your video app. The phone microphone works too with the free BlackHole audio driver. Video is never recorded.

Does it work with Samsung Galaxy and Pixel phones?

It works with any phone running Android 11 or newer over Wi‑Fi, and Android 8 to 10 over a USB cable. It's developed and tested daily on a Samsung Galaxy S23 Ultra.

macOS says it can't open the app. What now?

JH Mirror isn't on the App Store yet, so the first launch needs one confirmation: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. You only do it once.

Android turns Wireless debugging off after a restart. Do I pair again?

No. JH Mirror asks the phone to switch it back on over its encrypted channel and reconnects by itself, usually in about 20 seconds.

Does mirroring unlock my phone or weaken its security?

No. It keeps the phone awake while the mirror is open, but it never stores your PIN, never bypasses the lock screen and leaves your timeout and security settings as they were.
